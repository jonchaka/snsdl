How to export them

  1. Install a cookies.txt exporter in your browser: Get cookies.txt LOCALLY (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or cookies.txt (Firefox).
  2. Log in to the site. For YouTube, do it in a private window and close that window after exporting, so the session you export stays stable.
  3. On the site's page, click the extension and export for the current site in Netscape format. Pick the file or paste its contents below.
